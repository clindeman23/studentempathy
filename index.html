The Philosophy

The Tinkering Mentality 🔧

— Mitchel Resnick, MIT Media Lab
"The most important thing is not learning to code, but learning to think creatively, reason systematically, and work collaboratively — the skills needed to thrive."
Tinkering means jumping in before you have all the answers — poking, breaking, rebuilding, and finding joy in the messy middle of learning.
We built our whole lesson around this idea. No perfect outcomes required — just curious learners willing to try. 🙌
🧪
Experiment First
Try it before you understand it. Curiosity is the engine — not the manual.
🔁
Iterate Always
Version 1 is supposed to be broken. That's not failure — that's the process working.
💥
Embrace the Mess
Errors aren't problems — they're data. A wrong answer is just the next question.
🧠
Own Your Learning
Students drive. Teachers co-explore. Nobody in the room has all the answers.
The Heart of It

Empathy & Freedom to Fail 🫀

🫀
Start with Empathy
Before we could teach tinkering, we had to feel what our students felt — the frustration of not knowing, the embarrassment of being stuck. That awareness changed everything about how we taught.
🪞
Model the Struggle
We said "I don't know — let's figure it out together" out loud, on purpose. When teachers model confusion without shame, students see that not knowing is the starting point, not a failing.
🛡️
Create a Safe Space
Empathy lets you design for the student who is afraid to be wrong. Stations, choice, and peer collaboration all lower the stakes — so every student can take a real risk.
💥 Freedom to Fail is not a soft idea. It is a deliberate instructional choice — one that requires teachers to trust the process over the product.
🔄
Failure Is Feedback
When something doesn't work, that's information — not a verdict. We explicitly reframed errors as data: "What did you learn from that attempt? What would you change?"
🌱
The Plot Twist
The students who struggled the most often grew the most. The struggle was the point. Not an obstacle to learning — the very vehicle for it.
Tinkering + Empathy = students who believe they can figure it out — even when it's hard. 🔧🫀

Now It's Your Turn!

The 3 Stations 🎉

pick one & dive in!
⌨️ Station 1
Typing
Practice
Build up your typing speed and accuracy! Jump into a typing game or practice tool and see how fast your fingers can fly across the keyboard. Great for all skill levels — beginners and speed-demons welcome.
🎮 Compete with yourself
✂️ Station 2
Cricut
Keychain
Design and cut your own custom keychain using the Cricut machine! Pick a shape, add your name or a design, and walk away with something you made yourself. No experience needed — the machine does the cutting.
🎨 Take it home!
🤖 Station 3
Robot
Dogs
Get hands-on with the XGO robot dogs! Make them walk, dance, and do tricks using simple code blocks. Break something. Fix it. Try again. That's literally the point — this is what tinkering looks like in action.
💥 It's okay to break things
