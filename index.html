Chapter 1

Setting the Stage 🎭

It started with CodeCombat — a game-based coding platform where students wrote real Python to control a hero fighting through fantasy levels.
They learned to debug, to iterate, and that a broken program isn't failure — it's just a draft.
Then one day, we brought in something new that pushed those skills to a completely different level. 👀

Chapter 2

And Then We Got Dogs 🐶

🤖 The XGO Robot Dog

A four-legged programmable robot that works with micro:bit. Code it with MakeCode Blocks, Python, or JavaScript — each a different challenge level.

🎓 Student-Created Projects

Students designed goals, wrote their own programs, and presented what they wanted their robot dog to do. No hand-holding required.

Chapter 3

How to Teach Kids to Teach Dogs 🐾

The XGO was new to everyone — including the teachers. So we built a full guide to bridge skill gaps, support individual learners, and make the curriculum accessible to all.

  • 📊 Individual skill level — from blocks to Python
  • 💥 Individual willingness to fail — and grow from it
  • 📚 Helping teach curriculum — a living, linkable guide

Chapter 4

Feel the Burn 🔥

This is where empathy became essential. The robot dog code wasn't intuitive. Dogs fell off tables. Commands didn't work. And that was exactly the point. 🐾
We modeled our own confusion. We said "I don't know — let's figure it out together." And we meant it. 🤝
